Financial tips for students

Practical, honest money advice you can apply today.

50/30/20 rule

Spend 50% on needs, 30% on wants, and save 20% of your allowance every month.

Track every cent

Log every purchase. Small expenses add up faster than you think.

Smart groceries

Plan meals, buy in bulk where possible, and avoid shopping while hungry.

Transport savings

Look into student travel cards and share rides where it's safe and practical.

Student discounts

Always ask for student discounts. Save up to 20% on books, transport, and entertainment.

Emergency fund

Aim to save 3-6 months of expenses for unexpected situations. Start small.
