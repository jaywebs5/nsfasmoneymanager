💡 Daily Financial Tips

Smart money advice for students

50/30/20 Rule

Spend 50% on needs, 30% on wants, and save 20% of your allowance every month.

Track Every Cent

Use the expense tracker to log every purchase. Small expenses add up quickly!

Set SMART Goals

Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound goals help you stay focused.

Student Discounts

Always ask for student discounts. Save up to 20% on books, transport, and entertainment.

Emergency Fund

Save 3-6 months of expenses for unexpected situations. Start with R500 per month.

Review Monthly

Check your spending report every month. Identify areas where you can cut back.
